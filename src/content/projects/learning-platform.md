**The brief.** The team were asked to provide a walkthrough of the onboarding process before it was developed, to enable stakeholders and users (teachers) to provide feedback on the navigation – and to market the product months in advance of delivery.

## What we did

Stakeholders reviewed every line of the script for accuracy. We chose a warm, illustration style and a slow, reassuring pace. Key instructions appear on screen as well as in the narration. We organised live shoots with real teachers for the intro sections. The video was delivered with closed captions and an audio description. We also created a version with localised Spanish audio narration and assets.

## Outcome

The video was widely praised and proved a useful vehicle to get stakeholder and user feedback on the onboarding process. It led to further work creating a warm and friendly visual style across the entire learning platform.
