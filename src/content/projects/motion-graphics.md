**The brief.** Create instructional videos at scale for Math, ELA, Science and History learning programs aimed at the US K-12 market, for students aged 5-18.

## What we did

The team worked closely with learning experience designers to brainstorm and write scripts that aligned with specific learning goals for a particular module. After a storyboarded concept was approved a typical production process involved three rounds of edits on visual and an audio pass before getting sign off from stakeholders on a final output which was uploaded to a streaming platform. We also worked with dev team to ensure links to the videos were correctly embedded in the relevant lesson pages.

We deliberately created videos in a very wide range of visual styles including live and studio shoots, 2D and 3D animation, motion graphics, stop motion, papercraft and even Muppets created by the famous Jim Henson Workshop.

## Outcome
The videos were part of learning program used by over 50 million students in the US. In 2023 they won an Irish Animation Award for Best Animation for Apps, Games and Interactives.
