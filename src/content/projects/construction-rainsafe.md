**The brief.** Previous safety inductions relied on slide decks that site teams
switched off from. CubicM3 wanted video training with audio instructions and clear graphics that site workers would remember, along with metrics that would allow him to track who had viewed the safety instructions and when.

## What we did

We built a site in 3D using technical drawings and photographs from previous sites. Along with more generic assets such as excavators and terrain, we took special care to model the company's stormwater chambers to a high level of detail, and added company logos to on-screen characters to reinforce the branding.

## Outcome

The video provided an initial training output for site workers, with a mid-term plan to build stills into an interactive module with multiple choice questions, which could be more easily localised for EU markets.
