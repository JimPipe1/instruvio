**The brief.** The publisher were looking for a style that appealed to the target market: older, more experienced teachers.

## What we did

We settled a Mid-Century modern art style after sketching a variety of test samples, using a constricted color palette. The studio works with a number of illustrators /character designers / animators and can output work in a wide variety of styles to suit the project. In an age of AI-generated slop, hand-crafted artwork can be a key differentiator.

## Outcome

The book was published in 2026: [The Teaching Life](https://www.kellygallagher.org/books/the-teaching-life).
