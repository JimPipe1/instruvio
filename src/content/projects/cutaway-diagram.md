**The brief.** A Dublin-based manufacturer of stormwater management systems had a meeting with major new client and needed a clear visualisation of how they worked.

## What we did

We had already built out scenes showing a step by step build for the rainwater management system, so using 3D software we switched to a side elevation enabling a 'cutaway' view for a simple explainer video showing the system in action.

## Outcome

The video was one of multiple assets created for a marketing/sales deck presented to clients in combination with technical drawings and specifications. Showing clients 3D visualizations of construction methods clarifies the building process, uncovers design flaws early, reinforces the need for correct and safe installation, and builds trust before breaking ground.
