We make video for organisations that have something complicated to explain
and no in-house creative team to explain it. That might be a CPD module that
has to meet learning outcomes, a software workflow that customers keep getting
stuck on, or a public service that people need to understand before they can
use it.

## What we do

INSTRUVIO grew out of thirty years of making interactive media for e-learning and education. The team has a background in instructional design, animation, script-writing and media production at scale. We've built in a wide range of styles and formats, from motion graphics and 2D and 3D animation to web AR/VR and interactive training modules for CPD.

What has not changed in those thirty years is the hard part. The tools have
turned over completely — 3D scenes built in Blender, and AI doing in an
afternoon what used to take a week. The difficulty was never the tools. It was
deciding what to leave out.

That is still what we sell. A subject expert knows everything about their
subject, which is exactly what makes them the wrong person to explain it to a
beginner. Our job is to learn enough to ask awkward questions, tease out the
three things that actually matter, and build the video backwards from the
learning goal.

## Clarity first, then craft

A beautiful video that leaves the viewer unsure what to do next has failed.
Every project starts with the question *what should someone understand - and sometimes more importantly, feel – when this ends?* The script, visuals and pacing are built backwards from that answer.

We work closely with your subject experts, whether they are clinicians,
solicitors, engineers, product managers or policy teams. You bring the
knowledge. We handle everything that turns it into video.

## Everything included

- Scriptwriting, reviewed with your subject experts
- Storyboards and style frames before animation begins
- Custom illustration and motion graphics in your brand
- Professional voiceover, including Irish-accented and Irish-language (as Gaeilge) versions
- Licensed music and sound design
- Captions, transcripts and accessible design as standard
- Exports for web, social, presentations and your LMS
