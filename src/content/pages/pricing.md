Every project gets a fixed quote once we understand the brief, but nobody
wants to start a conversation with no idea of the number. Most of our work
falls into these ranges. All prices are in euro and exclude VAT.

## Indicative ranges

- **Explainer video**: 60 to 120 seconds of custom animation with script,
  voiceover and music. <!-- Typically €4,000 to €12,000 and four to six weeks.-->
- **CPD or training module**: 3 to 10 minutes per module, built around your
  learning outcomes. <!-- Typically €2,000 to €5,000 per module, with lower
  per-module pricing for a series.-->
- **SaaS onboarding and UI demos**: short, screen-led videos for in-app help,
  onboarding and feature releases. <!-- From €1,500 per video, or as a series.-->
- **Monthly video partnership**: a set amount of video each month for product,
  marketing or L&D teams with a steady flow of releases. <!-- From €3,500 per month.-->

## What moves the number

- **Length and complexity:** a two-minute animated explainer with custom
  characters costs more than a 60-second screen demo.
- **Illustration style:** bespoke illustration takes longer than a clean motion
  graphics style built from your brand assets.
- **Versions:** additional languages (including Irish), cut-downs for social,
  and alternative endings each add time.
- **Review rounds:** involving several subject experts or compliance sign-off
  lengthens the schedule. We plan for this from the start.

## What is always included

- Scriptwriting and storyboards
- Professional voiceover and licensed music
- Two rounds of changes at script, storyboard and animation stages
- Captions and a transcript file
- Exports for web, social and presentation, and an LMS-ready file for CPD work

## Public sector

We can quote against a tender specification or procurement framework, and
provide the tax clearance, insurance and accessibility documentation that
public bodies need.

Tell us what you need to explain and who needs to understand it, and we will
