**The brief.** A masterplan is read by people who do not read plans. The
consultation needed something that answered "what will this street feel like?"

## What we did

The quarter was built in 3D at two scales: an aerial pass that places the
development in the existing city grid, and street-level views at walking height
where the massing, shopfronts and planting can be judged the way a resident
would judge them.

Existing buildings were modelled alongside the new ones, so the film shows the
change in context rather than a new scheme floating on its own.
