Every job is quoted properly once we have seen your files, but nobody wants to
start a conversation with no idea of the number. These are the ranges most of
our work falls into.

## Indicative ranges

- **Short product loop** — 10 to 20 seconds, silent, for a product page.
  Typically £1,800 to £3,500 and around two weeks.
- **Installation sequence** — 60 to 120 seconds with commentary or captions.
  Typically £6,000 to £14,000 and four to six weeks.
- **Product stills** — a set of angles for one product, from £900. Whole
  ranges are cheaper per unit, because the setup is shared.
- **Technical details** — from £180 per detail, dropping sharply once there is
  a model to work from.

## What moves the number

The two things that matter most are whether usable 3D data already exists, and
how many separate deliverables come out at the end. A manufacturer with clean
Revit families and one animation to make sits at the bottom of these ranges. A
product that has to be modelled from paper drawings, feeding an animation plus
a catalogue plus a detail set, sits at the top.

## What does not cost extra

- The animatic stage, and the changes you ask for during it
- Re-exports at different sizes for web, stand and print
- Keeping the master model, so next year's variant is a small job

Send drawings and a rough idea of what you need it for, and you will get a
fixed price rather than a day rate.
