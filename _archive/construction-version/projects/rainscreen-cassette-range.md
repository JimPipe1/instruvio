Marden's finish range had grown to twenty-four options, and their sample
library had not kept up. Architects were specifying from a printed chart that
did not represent anodised or textured finishes at all well.

## What we did

Each finish was built as a measured material rather than a flat colour, so
anodised, powder-coated and textured options behave differently under the same
light — which is the whole point of choosing between them.

Every finish is then rendered on the same three profiles from the same angles,
producing a grid a specifier can scan quickly. Close crops cover the joint and
shadow gap, which is where a facade is judged from six feet away.

## Outcome

The finish library sits on Marden's website as a filterable grid. Sample
requests have not stopped, but they now arrive shortlisted to two or three
options rather than eight.
