Ilex had photographs of three units and nothing at all for the other six,
because those had never been built as stock. The catalogue looked
inconsistent, and the gaps were the units they most wanted to sell.

## What we did

We built the full range from the manufacturing models and rendered every unit
through the same camera, lens and lighting setup. Because it is the same setup
each time, a customer comparing a small unit with a large one is seeing a real
size difference rather than a difference in photography.

Everything was also delivered on transparent backgrounds, which let Ilex drop
units onto their own layouts and, later, feed the online configurator without
commissioning a second shoot.

## Outcome

The catalogue is now complete, including the units that have never physically
existed. New variants are added by rendering from the same scene, which takes
a day rather than a photography booking.
