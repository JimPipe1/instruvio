A method statement is read by people who need to get something right the first
time, often at height. Ilex wanted the drawings in theirs to be
unambiguous rather than merely present.

## What we did

We worked directly from the temporary works design, modelling the collar,
anchor plates and cast-in components at true dimensions. Nothing here is
stylised — where a diagram shows a bolt at a particular spacing, that is the
specified spacing.

Annotation lives on its own layer, so the same images serve the formal
document and a cleaner version used in the site induction.

## Outcome

The diagram set was accepted without comment by the principal contractor's
temporary works coordinator, and Ilex have since reused the format on two
further projects.
