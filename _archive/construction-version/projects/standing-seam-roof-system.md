Verity's details were drawn in 2D and had drifted out of step with the product
over about a decade. Some showed a fixing that had been superseded twice.

## What we did

We rebuilt every detail from one 3D model of the current system, which forced
the inconsistencies into the open early — three of the eighteen details turned
out to be describing a product Verity no longer sold.

Each layer sits at its specified thickness rather than an artistic
approximation, so a specifier measuring off the image gets the right answer.
Files were delivered with layers intact, so Verity's own team can switch
labelling on and off for different documents.

## Outcome

The detail set now runs through the technical handbook, the NBS specification
and the website, all from one source. When the fixing changes next, one model
is updated and every image is re-exported.
