A hoarding is often the only thing the public sees of a scheme for two years,
and it goes up long before there is anything to photograph.

## What we did

We built the street context from survey data and site photography, then placed
the proposed building into it at the correct height and orientation, with
light matched to the aspect each panel faces. A panel on a north-facing
elevation showing bright southern sun looks wrong even to people who could not
say why.

Artwork was delivered at final print size, checked at 1:1 for the viewing
distance rather than at fit-to-screen.

## Outcome

The panels went up in advance of the enabling works and were reused by the
developer for the scheme's planning and marketing material.
