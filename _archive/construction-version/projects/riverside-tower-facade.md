Northbank were losing tenders to competitors whose systems looked simpler to
install, even where they were not. The problem was not the product — it was that
nobody could see how it went together from a drawing.

## What we did

We modelled the unitised panel and its bracket from Northbank's own Revit
family, then rebuilt the sequence exactly as the site team runs it: set out,
crane, hook, plumb, seal. The animation runs at the same pace as the real
install, so a contractor can watch it and recognise their own working day.

The transparent-frame cutaway was the piece that mattered most. It shows the
bracket taking the load at the point where every estimator asks the question,
which turned a long phone call into a fifteen-second clip.

## Outcome

The sequence now opens every Northbank pitch, plays on a loop on their trade
stand, and is embedded on the product page. Their sales team report that the
first meeting with a new main contractor is roughly half as long as it was.
