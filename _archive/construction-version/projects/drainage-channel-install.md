Cawley were replacing channels that had failed early, and the pattern in the
claims was clear: the concrete surround was being skimped where it mattered.
Telling installers this in a PDF was not working.

## What we did

We made a two-minute animation that shows the right method and, briefly, the
wrong one — the section view makes the consequence of a thin surround obvious
in a way a paragraph of text does not.

The video is deliberately short and works with the sound off, because it is
watched on a phone, outdoors, standing in a trench. A QR code on the packaging
links straight to it.

## Outcome

Cawley report a marked drop in bedding-related claims in the year since. The
same stills are now used in their toolbox talks.
